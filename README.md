# Unraid templates

Unraid Community Applications templates for the apps I maintain.

| App | What it does | Template |
|---|---|---|
| [AutoThrottle](https://github.com/JaiMistry/autothrottle) | Lowers qBittorrent or Transmission speed limits while someone is watching on Plex, Jellyfin or Emby, and puts them back when they stop. | [autothrottle.xml](templates/autothrottle.xml) |

## Installing

Search for the app in Unraid's **Apps** tab and click **Install**. The
defaults work as they are: the WebUI is on port 7676, and settings are kept
in `/mnt/user/appdata/autothrottle`.

## Support

Open an issue on the app's own repository, linked above. Issues with a
template itself (a wrong default, a missing setting) are welcome here.
